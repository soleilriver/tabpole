敲两下背面不记账？Back Tap not working

打开 App → 设置 → “设置‘付完敲两下就记账’”，按步骤重新设置一次快捷指令；再到 iPhone 设置 → 辅助功能 → 触控 → 轻点背面，确认“轻点两下”选的是这个快捷指令。

In the app, go to Settings → Set up tap-to-log and redo the shortcut. Then check iPhone Settings → Accessibility → Touch → Back Tap, and make sure Double Tap runs that shortcut.

汇率不对 / 显示“待换算”Rates pending

外币账单要联网拿到当天汇率后才会换算。连上网再打开 App，会自动补上。

Foreign-currency expenses are converted once the app can download that day's rate. Open the app while online and they fill in automatically.

账本用什么币种？Book currency

每个账本可以有自己的结算币种，比如日常用美元、中国旅行用人民币。在账本右上角的菜单里点“账本设置”就能改。

Each book has its own currency — e.g. USD for everyday, CNY for a China trip. Change it in the book's menu → Book settings.

换手机 / 备份Backup & new phone

数据只存在手机上。在设置 → 备份里点“生成备份文件”，到新手机上用“从备份恢复”导入即可。App 也会每天在“文件 → 我的 iPhone → Tabpole → Backups”自动存一份。

Your data lives only on your phone. In Settings → Backup, tap Create backup file, then use Restore from backup on the new phone. The app also saves a daily copy in Files → On My iPhone → Tabpole → Backups.
