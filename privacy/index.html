隐私政策Privacy Policy

生效日期 Effective · 2026-10-26

你记的账、截图、照片和朋友名单都只存在你自己的 iPhone 上。我们没有服务器，不收集、不上传、不出售任何个人信息。

Your expenses, screenshots, photos and friend list stay on your own iPhone. We run no servers and do not collect, upload or sell any personal information.

存在哪里Where data lives

所有账本、账单、分摊、还款记录、截图和照片都保存在 App 的本地数据库里。App 每天会在“文件 → 我的 iPhone → Tabpole → Backups”自动保存一份备份（保留最近 7 份），同样只在你的设备上。删除 App 会一起删除这些数据和备份。

All books, expenses, splits, repayments, screenshots and photos are stored in the app's local database. The app also saves a daily backup (last 7 kept) in Files → On My iPhone → Tabpole → Backups, also on your device only. Deleting the app deletes this data and its backups.

截图识别Screenshot recognition

识别截图里的金额、商户和酒店信息，是用苹果系统自带的文字识别（Vision）在手机本地完成的，截图不会发到任何地方。

Amounts, merchants and hotel details are read from screenshots on your device using Apple's built-in text recognition (Vision). Screenshots are never sent anywhere.

联网只为了汇率Network: rates only

App 联网时只做一件事：从下面的公开汇率服务下载最新汇率。请求里不包含你的任何账单或个人信息。和访问任何网站一样，这些服务可能会看到你的 IP 地址。

  • open.er-api.com（ExchangeRate-API）
  • api.frankfurter.app（Frankfurter）

The only network use is downloading current exchange rates from the public services above. Requests contain none of your expenses or personal information. As with any website, these services may see your IP address.

不做的事What we don't do

没有账号和登录，没有广告，没有统计分析或崩溃上报工具，不跟踪你，不和任何第三方共享数据。

No accounts or sign-in, no ads, no analytics or crash reporting, no tracking, and no sharing of data with third parties.

权限Permissions

  • 添加照片到相册：只在你点“存相册”时，保存你生成的海报或结算单。
  • 选择照片：你从相册挑截图或照片时，只读取你选中的那几张。
  • 快捷指令 / Apple Pay 交易自动化：只有你自己在“快捷指令”里设置后才会运行，传给 App 的只有金额、商家和卡片名称，同样只存在手机上。

Add to Photos is used only when you tap Save, to store posters or settle-up cards you created. Photo picker reads only the images you select. Shortcuts and Apple Pay automations run only if you set them up; they pass the amount, merchant and card name, which stay on your device.

导出与分享Export & sharing

导出表格、手动备份、分享结算单或海报，都是你主动操作的。文件发到哪里（iCloud 云盘、微信、邮件等）由你选择，之后由那个服务按它自己的隐私政策处理。

Exports, manual backups and sharing settle-up cards or posters only happen when you choose. You decide where they go (iCloud Drive, a messaging app, email…), and that service handles them under its own privacy policy.

儿童Children

本 App 不会有意收集任何人的个人信息，包括 13 岁以下的儿童。

The app does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children under 13.

政策更新Changes

如果以后增加了会联网同步或收集数据的功能（比如 iCloud 同步），我们会先更新这一页，并修改上面的生效日期。

If a future version adds syncing or any data collection (such as iCloud sync), this page will be updated first and the effective date will change.
